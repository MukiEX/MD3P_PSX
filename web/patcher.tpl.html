What changes

  • The clown follows a virtual paddle, one column per tick, through the game's own movement code, so sounds and animations stay.
  • The clown and aim line glide between columns, and the column you point at pulses.
  • Over a column whose bottom balloon matches what you hold, the mouse slows to half speed and the NeGcon gets sticky.
  • The PS1 reads pads once per frame, so a JP3 spinner keeps up to 2 edges per frame (100 or 120 per second); faster turns need an adapter that paces its edges.
  • Options gains a MOUSE SPEED row. Card select, name entry and the language screen accept the mouse and the NeGcon.
  • All hidden characters are unlocked. All six languages are patched.
